PRODUCTION SEQUENCE
Purpose before technique
Every project moves through the same six steps. Each step maps to a stage you can watch in the project log.

        PRINCIPLES v1.0
      

      

        
01
Define
Main message, audience, desired response and delivery format. OMNIA derives a delivery brief and labels every guess as an assumption.

        
02
Plan
Narration written for the ear in thought groups, with numbers, dates and names flagged for a chosen spoken reading before any voice is made.

        
03
Capture or generate
Your media plus matched B-roll, music and effects. Every asset is validated and assigned a role it must serve.

        
04
Assemble
A sequence built around meaning and progression, cut to the measured voice — never to an estimate.

        
05
Refine
Clean cuts over flashy transitions, ducked music under speech, readable captions in the safe zone.

        
06
Finish & verify
The exported file is measured and scored HOOK → CTA. What code can't judge is handed to you, not auto-passed.
